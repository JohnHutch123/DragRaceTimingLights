# Drag Tree privacy policy

_Last updated: 21 October 2026_

Drag Tree is a drag racing start-tree trainer for practising reaction times.

**Drag Tree does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.**

- The app works offline and does not connect to the internet.
- It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
- Your settings are saved only on your own device, in the app's private
  storage. They are removed when you uninstall the app.
- The app does not ask for access to your location, camera, contacts, microphone
  or files.

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the new version is released.
